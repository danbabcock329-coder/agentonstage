## What it does

ShowOps.AI's Produce module keeps a single schedule across departments from load-in to strike. It flags conflicts such as overlapping dock use and creates call sheets once staffing is confirmed.

## How to set it up

1. Load departments, spaces and load-in-to-strike tasks into Produce.
2. Connect venue readiness and staffing rosters so schedule constraints and call sheets follow them.
3. Review flagged conflicts (e.g. AV lift and catering sharing a dock) and resolve them.
