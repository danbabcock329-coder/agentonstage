## What it does

Light Assistant builds cue sheets, spot sheets, hookups and schedules. During tech it follows Eos consoles over OSC so notes stay attached to the right cue.

## How to set it up

1. Build the cue sheet as a storyboard, then sync labels, scene breaks and page numbers to Eos.
2. Exchange instrument data with Vectorworks when you choose to sync.
3. During tech let it follow Eos over OSC and take notes that link to cues and fixtures.
4. Export PDFs (cue sheets, hookups, color cuts) for the crew.

Source: [Light Assistant vendor site](https://lightassistant.com/)

## FAQ

### Is there a free tier?
Yes. There's a limited free version and a web app, alongside subscription and perpetual plans.

### Is it AI?
It's automation, not an LLM, but it's a solid data source for agent-written paperwork.
