## What it does

The standard open-source shotbox for production gear, and a common trigger point for automations.

## How to set it up

1. Download Companion from bitfocus.io/companion (user guide at companion.free).
2. Add connections for your gear (700+ modules) and build button pages.
3. Use Triggers and OSC/HTTP remote control so other systems, including agents, can press buttons.

Source project: [bitfocus/companion](https://github.com/bitfocus/companion)

## FAQ

### Do I need a Stream Deck?
No. There's a built-in emulator, a web page for touch screens and OSC/TCP/UDP/HTTP/WebSocket/Art-Net triggers.

### Cost?
Free and open source; donations go via Open Collective.
